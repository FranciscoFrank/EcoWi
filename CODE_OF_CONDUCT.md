# Code of Conduct

EcoWi is a community project. We want everyone who uses it, reports issues or contributes, whatever their experience, background or identity, to feel welcome and respected.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that help:

- Being kind, patient and constructive, especially with newcomers.
- Respecting different viewpoints, and disagreeing about ideas rather than people.
- Giving and gracefully accepting feedback, and owning our mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people who use it.
- Protecting other people's privacy, for example by redacting serial numbers and e-mails in shared logs.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information (addresses, e-mails, credentials, device identifiers) without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, reviews) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer, [@FranciscoFrank](https://github.com/FranciscoFrank). If you can't find a private way to reach them, open an issue that only asks for a private contact, without any details. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will review every report and respond in a way they consider appropriate, which may include:

1. **A private note** explaining why the behaviour was inappropriate.
2. **A warning**, with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **A temporary ban** from interacting with the project.
4. **A permanent ban** for serious or repeated violations.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code.
